# Code of conduct

Ferry follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming in issues, pull requests and discussions, including to people who are new to open source or who write in their second language. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people, who break this code.

To report a comment, use **Report content** in its **⋯** menu, or contact the maintainer, [@tanzeel152](https://github.com/tanzeel152), privately. Reports are kept confidential.
